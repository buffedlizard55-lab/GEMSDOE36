## Interpretation

1. The conjugate-scarp and coherent-scarp features carry signal on their own (single-feature AUC
   up to 0.58, comparable to the strongest raw LiDAR bands) but **no information beyond what the
   model already extracts** from `downface_max`, `upface_max`, `step_max`, `coh100` and the
   multi-scale topographic context: the paired delta is centred slightly below zero and its CI
   spans zero (2/4 folds).
2. As a stand-alone, label-free detector the physics is weak (0.054 vs 0.134): conjugate pairs are
   a small subset of mapped faults, and mimics (beach ridges, embankments) are not removed.
3. Neither arm reaches the recorded group best 0.14876, so by the preregistered rule the lane is
   **NEGATIVE** and its GeoTIFF is **DOWNLOAD: YES · SUBMIT: NO**.
4. Caveat that cuts both ways: the holdout truth is the mapped catalogue; expert-labelled new faults
   may be smaller scarps for which polarity pairing matters more. That cannot be tested without an
   organizer receipt, and a slot must not be spent on an idea that failed the holdout gate.

## What not to repeat

* Adding hand-built LiDAR geometry features to a gradient-boosted model that already sees the raw
  LiDAR bands (E1).
* Using the LiDAR product's `strike` band as fault strike (IR-22).
* Per-pixel learners alone did not beat the hand-built H19-5 field live in GEMSDOE32 either; B0's
  0.134 is the same level as GEMSDOE24's reconstructed OOF baseline (0.130).
