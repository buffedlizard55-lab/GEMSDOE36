## Scientific interpretation guardrails

- The contest target is fault geometry, not a geothermal probability map. A heat-flow or spring layer is context, not fault truth.
- The official feature description lists scalar strain summaries. Do not infer a unique stress direction from dilatation, shear magnitude, or a second invariant alone.
- Regional extension is not uniform across the Great Basin/Walker Lane. Use spatially varying priors, mixed-regime uncertainty, and a weak training loss—not a hard compass filter.
- A geophysical lineament may be a lithologic contact, survey artifact, or non-fault structure. Require multiscale persistence, cross-layer evidence, spatial holdouts, and manual geological review before promoting it.
- Search-result snippets and public repository notes are discovery aids, not organizer evidence for this repository's score or file attribution.
