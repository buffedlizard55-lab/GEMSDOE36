## 2. PhD-Level Explanation: Why & How `GEMSDOE32`'s `h33-h33-2-b2-20261004T220000Z-e5eb6e7e-zeros` Achieved `0.2778 OWNER-REPORTED` — and How to Bridge Toward `0.3195` and `0.4413`

### 2.1 Exact Fractional-Linear Mechanics of the Distance-Weighted Tversky Index (DTI)
On the active unmasked evaluation domain $\Omega^* = \text{Footprint} \setminus C_{\text{known}}$ ($|\Omega^*| = 5{,}167{,}373 - 60{,}988 = 5{,}106{,}385$ cells at $100\,\text{m}$ resolution), let $G \subset \Omega^*$ denote the hidden expert-mapped fault pixel set of unknown cardinality $K = |G|$, and let $P \subset \Omega^*$ denote a binary submission of $N = |P|$ predicted dots. Under the $R = 3.0\,\text{px}$ ($300\,\text{m}$) Euclidean triangular proximity kernel $w(d) = \max(0, 1 - d / 3.0)$:

$$\text{TP}_w = \sum_{g \in G} w(d(g, P)), \qquad \text{FN}_w = K - \text{TP}_w, \qquad \text{FP}_w = \sum_{p \in P} \bigl(1 - w(d(p, G))\bigr)$$

Because $\text{TP}_w + \beta\,\text{FN}_w = (1 - \beta)\,\text{TP}_w + \beta K = 0.2\,\text{TP}_w + 0.8 K$ for $(\alpha, \beta) = (0.2, 0.8)$, the exact competition score reduces to:

$$\text{DTI}(P) = \frac{\text{TP}_w}{\text{TP}_w + 0.2\,\text{FP}_w + 0.8\,\text{FN}_w} = \frac{\text{TP}_w}{0.2\,\text{TP}_w + 0.2\,\text{FP}_w + 0.8 K}$$

Differentiating $\text{DTI}(P)$ with respect to adding one candidate dot $p$ that contributes marginal recall gain $\Delta\text{TP}_w$ and incurs false-positive penalty $\Delta\text{FP}_w \le 1$ yields the **exact marginal break-even inequality**:

$$\Delta\text{DTI} > 0 \iff \frac{\Delta\text{TP}_w}{0.2\,(\Delta\text{TP}_w + \Delta\text{FP}_w)} > \text{DTI} \iff \Delta\text{TP}_w > \frac{0.2\,\text{DTI}}{1 - 0.2\,\text{DTI}}\,\Delta\text{FP}_w \approx 0.2\,\text{DTI}$$

At $\text{DTI} = 0.2778$, a single isolated dot ($\Delta\text{FP}_w \approx 1$) improves the score **if and only if** its expected marginal weighted recall $\mathbb{E}[\Delta\text{TP}_w] > 0.0556$ hidden truth pixels!

**Why each operator produced those exact jumps:**
1. **Continuous Ridge $\to$ Dotted Chain (`+0.0678` total from `0.1922` to `0.2600`):** On a contiguous 1-pixel predicted line of length $L$ that misses a hidden fault, all $L$ pixels incur $\text{FP}_w = L$. Subsampling every $2.8\,\text{px}$ cuts `FP_w` by **$3.0\times$** (`129,184` $\to$ `44,090` dots), while on true faults the $3.0\,\text{px}$ triangular kernel still bridges adjacent dots ($w(1.4\,\text{px}) = 0.533$, mean along-trace recall retention $\approx 74\%$). Because $\alpha = 0.2$ multiplies `FP_w` across `~40,000` off-target dots ($\Delta(0.2\,\text{FP}_w) \approx -17{,}000$), denominator shrinkage far outweighs the modest numerator dip.
2. **Removing the `6,436` Near-Catalogue Echo Dots (`+0.0178` from `0.2600` to `0.2778`):** DrivenData staff confirmed on the competition forum that known `existing_faults.tif` pixels are masked **pixel-exactly** (`100 m` cell only), NOT buffered. However, `H19-5`'s smoothed 100 m topographic/magnetic filters produce a broad `200–300 m` detector-response halo around mapped USGS faults. Consequently, `6,436` dots in `D2.8` sat at $100\text{–}200\,\text{m}$ from already-mapped faults — overwhelmingly **detector bleed from known faults** rather than genuine parallel hidden fault strands. Deleting those `6,436` echo dots (`44,090` $\to$ `37,654`) subtracted $\approx 0.2 \times 5{,}800 \approx 1{,}160$ denominator penalty units at negligible `TP_w` cost, lifting `0.2600` $\to$ `0.2778`. (Caveat: without hidden labels or organizer submission receipts, exact `TP_w`/`FP_w` decomposition remains an analytical inference, not an observed identity.)

### 2.3 Why Re-Thinning `H19-5` Asymptotes Below `~0.295` — and What Physically Separates `0.2778` from `0.3195` and `0.4413+`

Reaching **`0.3195`** and **`0.4413+`** requires **expanding recall (`TP_w`) onto unmapped faults that `H19-5` never detected**, while maintaining axial 1-pixel precision:

Consolidating multi-domain Stage-1 maps via 2D Hessian/DoG/worm spatial context collapses halos onto 1-pixel fault spines so `min_spacing_px >= 2.5–2.9` places single-file dot chains without 2D blob waste.
4. **Bedrock Structural Prior Synthesis (Beyond Current Sandbox Rasters):** Public leaderboard leaders above `0.40+` likely incorporate complete 1:24k–1:100k NBMG/USGS pre-Quaternary bedrock fault/contact vector geometry off-catalogue ($d_{\text{cat}} \ge 3$) or high-resolution Sentinel-1/2 & ASTER TIR alteration mosaics where hidden expert traces follow mapped Cenozoic bedrock faults not yet promoted into the Quaternary `existing_faults.tif` layer.
