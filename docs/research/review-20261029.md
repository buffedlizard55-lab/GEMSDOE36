## Pinned-content audit — duplicate groups and exact scope

The audit checked **10 TIFFs**, not just nine: the nine pinned historical-best rasters plus GEMSDOE28's additional h27-4 family comparator. Among those artifacts it found **eight distinct in-footprint contents**. Full hashes, filenames, dot counts, and outside encodings are in [`focus9_content_checks_20261028.json`](../../evidence/focus9_content_checks_20261028.json).

1. **GEMSDOE25 / GEMSDOE30:** same 44,090 positive pixels and identical in-footprint values. Their full-file hashes differ because GEMSDOE25 has NaNs outside the prediction footprint while GEMSDOE30 encodes zeros there.
2. **GEMSDOE28 alternate / GEMSDOE31:** `gems28-h27-4-r1-solo-d2-8-20261003-8acb75e1f2cc-nan.tif` and `gemsdoe31-h27-4-solo-d28-20261004-8acb75e1-nan.tif` have identical in-footprint content (40,199 dots each). The GEMSDOE28 member is the **h27-4 alternate**, not GEMSDOE28's **h36-1 historical-best**, which has 37,660 dots.

A separate pinned-byte lineage check shows that GEMSDOE32 B2 (37,654 dots) is a strict subset of the 44,090-dot D2.8 family raster: **6,436 dots removed, zero added**. The removed dots are 1–2 pixels from the catalogue; no kept B2 dot is within 2 pixels. This is a verified raster relationship, not a verified cause of the owner-reported score.

## Registry gate and decision

The local `reg-052` reference is a dense regular lattice. Its 3-pixel neighborhood covers **5,160,724 / 5,167,373** footprint pixels (99.8713%). For a 37,654-dot candidate, a pigeonhole bound forces at least **31,005 dots (82.3418%)** within 3 pixels. The threshold is forced above 70% from **22,164 dots** upward.

This proves that the literal test is non-discriminating for ordinary candidate densities against this reference. It does **not** prove an unseen, scientifically different detector is a duplicate. No threshold was changed and no candidate was generated to bypass the gate. The surface-rank-correlation and final-dot checks were not run for a new candidate because none exists. The comparison rasters are SHA-pinned owner/GitHub copies, not organizer-authenticated registry data.
