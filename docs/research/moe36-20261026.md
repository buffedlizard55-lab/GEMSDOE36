## 2. Why the owner-reported 0.2778 is plausible (mask algebra measured; blind score cause unknown)

1. The repository's local evaluator uses Tversky α=0.2, β=0.8 with a 300 m triangular kernel. The [official metric page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) needs independent manual review; no authenticated portal scoring is claimed. Earlier owner-score-based inversions suggest that dots ≤2 px (200 m) from the catalogue could have lower credit (~0.002–0.006/dot versus ~0.130 for a kept dot), but that “dead annulus” inference depends on unverified score/file pairings and hidden-truth assumptions.
2. Measured from pinned rasters, `h33-h33-2-b2` added **zero new coordinates**: it is exactly
   `d2.8 (44,090 dots, 0.2600)` minus the 6,436 dots within 2 px of the
   catalogue (0 XOR verified). The owner-reported progression 0.2600 → 0.2708 (B=1 px) → **0.2778** (B=2 px) is consistent with reducing false positives, but is not proof of blind-score causality.
3. A reciprocal-Tversky inversion across owner-reported scores gives a **conditional** K ≈ 12,853 px under assumptions about the hidden set and metric. Actual blind truth, current public score and required recall are unknown. This is not a target or promotion gate.
