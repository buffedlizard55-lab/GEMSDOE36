# Pre-Registration: Lane ATVR-36 (2026-10-26 UTC)

**Locked BEFORE any holdout model training or evaluation.**

- **Lane:** `ATVR-36` — *Andersonian Tilt-Variational & Deconfounded Geothermal-Piedmont Submodular Fault Discovery*
- **Evaluator:** `gems36-segment-audit-v1` (`src/gemsdoe36/protocol.py` + `src/gemsdoe36/metric.py`)
- **Holdout Design:** Whole 8-connected fault segments (`3,199` segments, `60,988` positive catalogue pixels) withheld across 4 geographic quadrants (`NW`, `NE`, `SW`, `SE`). Any segment touching quadrant $k$ is withheld in its entirety, with a `30 px` (`3.0 km`) training exclusion collar around `quad_k | hidden_k`. Every catalogue-derived quantity (training positives, `d_vis > 4 px` clean negative sampling buffer, regime prevalence calibration, Anderson `local_extension_field`, and `d_vis > 2.0 px` flank exclusion) is computed strictly from fold-visible faults (`visible_k`). Unwithheld visible faults are masked pixel-exactly. Pooled `HOLDOUT-DTI` (`alpha=0.2, beta=0.8, 300 m` triangular kernel) is computed with a 16-block whole-spatial-cell bootstrap `95% CI` (`2,000` replicates, `seed=36`) and cross-checked against unsplit 4-quadrant pooled `DTI`.
- **Banned Inputs (Hard Invariant):** Zero prior submission GeoTIFF pixels; zero `derived_sgmc_faults_100m_u8.tif`; zero `dist_known_fault_px` column in GDR 1391; zero `gdr_qfaults_traces.csv`; zero `sample_submission.tif` pixel values (footprint finite mask only).
- **Budget:** Maximum `3` experiments (`EXP-1`, `EXP-2`, `EXP-3`) against two frozen baselines (`C0_repo_field` and `B1_raw19_hgb_d28`), all evaluated at the identical `37,654` off-visible-catalogue dot budget.
