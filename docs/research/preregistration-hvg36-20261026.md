## 1. Motivation & Previous-Session Next Steps Executed First

Across prior `GEMSDOE36` lanes evaluated on the shared `gems-segment-pooled-v1` benchmark (`60,988` withheld positives):
- `H36-GPS` baseline `B0` (59 multi-scale DEM/LiDAR/GeoDAWN features + ridge-NMS greedy 3-px dot emitter) achieved **`HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.133749`** (`95% CI [0.122241, 0.146035]`), with `NW = 0.14736`, `NE_LidarGapHeavy = 0.17293`, `SW = 0.08798`, `SE = 0.14836`.
- `ATVR-36 EXP-2` (hydrothermal + Verduzco tilt + piedmont scarp-toe curvature + Anderson variational diffusion) achieved **`NW = 0.15048`, `SW = 0.11115`, `SE = 0.15438`**, outperforming `B0` in 3 of 4 folds, but dropped to `0.05312` in `NE_LidarGapHeavy` (pooled `0.117925`) because global submodular allocation placed only `906` of `37,654` dots in the LiDAR-sparse `NE` quadrant.
- `MOE36 EXP-1` (`HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.124900`) showed that cross-expert fusion significantly beats individual experts (`+0.010115`, paired 95% CI `[+0.0017, +0.0182]`), but rank-flattening probabilities to `U(0, 1)` before submodular emission and omitting the 59-feature `B0` expert kept it below `B0 = 0.133749`.
- `MTO-36` (`HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.067611`) tested magnetic-contact termination $\times$ orthogonal gravity (Rank 1 in `knowledge/mtop36_preregistration_2026-10-26.md`) and left Rank 2 (`P-TMI`), Rank 3 (`D-PAIR`), and Rank 4 (`RAD-ANISO`) untested.
- `WDP-36` (`HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.093269`) built an unsupervised 0–800 m upward-continuation worm-persistence surface with low dot overlap against all 9 focus sites (`max = 0.4909`, `0.2360` vs `GEMSDOE32` `B2`), and explicitly recommended a preregistered cross-lane out-of-fold ensemble as the #1 next step.

## 3. Experimental Protocol & Promotion Gates (Max 3 Experiments, Max 2 Hours)

1. **Shared Holdout & Control:**
   - Evaluator: `gems.holdout.SegmentHoldout` (`gems-segment-pooled-v1`, `buffer_px = 15`, `3,199` whole components, `60,988` withheld positives, per-fold budgets `[8414, 10404, 10410, 8426]` summing to `37,654` dots).
   - Matched Baseline: `B0` (`59` base features, `HOLDOUT-DTI = 0.133749`, `95% CI [0.122241, 0.146035]`).
   - Recorded Group Best on `gems-segment-pooled-v1`: `0.14876` (`GEMSDOE24` `H24-GVC1`, `95% CI [0.13413, 0.16370]`).
2. **Three Locked Experiments:**
   - **`EXP-1` (`HVG-Single-73`):** Single out-of-fold HGB on `59 B0 + 14 HVG-36` features (`73` features total) + `emit_dots` (`sigma=1.0, block=2`).
   - **`EXP-2` (`HVG-DualExpert-ProbFuse`):** Probability-space geometric fusion of out-of-fold `E_BASE59` (59 features) and LiDAR-free `E_HVG_SUBSURF` (39 subsurface/geothermal/dipole/piedmont features) with training-fold LiDAR-gap tail calibration + `emit_dots` (`sigma=1.0, block=2`).
   - **`EXP-3` (`HVG-TriExpert-B2-Collar`):** Probability-space fusion of `E_BASE59 + E_HVG_SUBSURF + E_WDP_PERSIST` (adding unsupervised 0–800 m upward-continuation worm persistence) with strict `d_vis > 2 px` (`200 m` visible-catalogue collar exclusion) and `emit_dots` (`sigma=1.0, block=2`).
3. **Hard Promotion Gates (Fail-Closed):**
   - **Gate G1 (Matched Control Lift):** Winning arm must beat matched `B0` (`0.133749`) on pooled `HOLDOUT-DTI` with paired 10 km block-bootstrap 95% CI strictly above `0.0` (`ci95_strictly_positive = True`) and win $\ge 3/4$ folds.
   - **Gate G2 (Current Holdout Best):** Winning arm must exceed the recorded comparable group best (`0.14876` on `gems-segment-pooled-v1`).
   - **Gate G3 (Leakage Canary):** Every individual feature must have two-polarity ROC-AUC $\le 0.90$ across all folds and pooled (`holdout.canary`).
   - **Gate G4 (Registry Uniqueness):** Pre-placement surface `max |Spearman rho| <= 0.90` AND final dots `max 3-px dot overlap <= 0.70` across the registry (`compare_registry`; both focus-9 / competing-method and literal all-203 registry outcomes reported transparently; if literal overlap exceeds `0.70` on any registry raster, log it as a duplicate-flag and set `verdict = "negative"` / `DO_NOT_SUBMIT`).
   - **Gate G5 (Format & Strict `[0, 1]` Range):** Single-band `float32`, `EPSG:32611`, `3730 x 3292`, `nodata = None`, zero `NaN`/`Inf` across all `12,279,160` cells, all values in `[0.0, 1.0]`, zero predictions on `existing_faults.tif`.
