### Why GEMSDOE32 may have scored highest (careful, non-causal explanation)

Sparse predictions can improve distance-weighted Tversky-style objectives when each added positive has diminishing spatial credit but still increases false-positive cost. Removing points near already-known catalogue faults can also help if the organizer excludes those regions from evaluation. The supplied site description and repo's archived narrative are consistent with that explanation. They do **not** establish that those operations caused the score difference: the rasters, evaluator, fold definitions, masks, and receipts have not been independently controlled and compared here. Nor does fault-map score demonstrate geothermal-vent discovery, which is a distinct scientific/application claim.

It is possible in principle to beat 0.2778; there is no valid current evidence that this checkout can. The supplied 0.3195 maximum is likewise not verified here and is not a target converted into a projection.
