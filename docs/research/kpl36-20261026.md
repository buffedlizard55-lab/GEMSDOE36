## 1. Why and how 0.2778 got the family's highest score (line-by-line, from the bytes)

Re-verified this session from the fetched files (all 97 focus-9 rasters restored from their
pinned GitHub commits, SHA-256 match 97/97):

1. **One family, nine sites.** Every site champion is a thinning/subset of the 19GEMSDOE
   H19-5 corroborated field (121,131 px, 0.1922 OWNER-REPORTED). Measured pairwise 3-px
   dot overlap between the 9 champions is **0.87–1.00** — under the protocol's
   ≤0.70 uniqueness rule, *no* champion is unique against *any* other.
2. **GEMSDOE25 d2.8 ≡ GEMSDOE30 poisson300m** (44,090 dots, both 0.2600): identical
   in-footprint mask, XOR = 0. Same file, two sites, same score (scorer deterministic).
3. **GEMSDOE31/28 solo** (40,199 dots, 0.2708) = d2.8 minus dots ≤1 px from the catalogue.
4. **GEMSDOE32 B2** (37,654 dots, **0.2778**) = d2.8 minus the 6,436 dots ≤2 px from the
   catalogue. This session recomputed it: `(d2.8 ∧ d_cat>2) XOR B2 = 0`, exactly.
5. **GEMSDOE36 Anderson** (38,854 dots, 0.2750) = B2 plus 1,200 extra dots (B2 ⊂ Anderson,
   verified: 100% of B2 dots are in Anderson). Those 1,200 dots earned ≈0.014 credit each —
   below the 0.0556 break-even at DTI 0.2778 — so the score *fell* to 0.2750.
6. **Mechanism (PhD-level reading).** The official metric obeys
   `DTI = T / (0.2(T+S−M) + 0.8N)` (300 m triangular kernel, α=0.2, β=0.8;
   https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/).
   A dot is profitable iff its realised kernel credit exceeds `0.2·DTI`. Hidden truth is
   *new faults not in the USGS database* and known faults are scorer-masked, so dots
   within ~200 m of a mapped fault earn almost nothing (measured: 0.0018–0.0059/dot for the
   deleted rings vs 0.130/dot champion average; `why-02778.md`). 0.2778 is therefore
   **denominator thrift on the best historical field — no new geology after 0.2600.**
7. **What beating it requires.** At 37,654 dots and effective truth N′≈12,541, mean
   per-dot credit must rise from 0.130 to ≈0.141 (+8%) for 0.30 and ≈0.206 (+59%) for
   0.4437. Emission tricks on the same field are exhausted (B-ladder gains were
   +0.0108, +0.0070 and shrinking); only a better detector of *unmapped* faults moves it.
