## What the nine repositories teach

Commit-pinned snapshots of GEMSDOE24, 25, 27, 28, 30, 31, 32, 33 and 36
are stored in `evidence/review-20261025/`; `sources.json` records the exact commits.
These are participant claims, not official leaderboard receipts. The retrieved
GEMSDOE32 page itself contains the contradictory label “ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED,
owner-reported”; that is insufficient to call a result organizer-confirmed.
The supplied leaderboard values and competing current-high claims are unverified.
Do not turn any of them into a local score or a predicted live score.

GEMSDOE32 describes a nested sparse-emission family with catalogue-flank pruning.
That supports a plausible explanation: thinning redundant predictions and removing
low-credit dots can lower false-positive mass without proportionately reducing
recovered truth. This is scoring geometry, not proof of vent discovery. Exact
causation requires controlled comparisons against unseen labels, not leaderboard
comparisons among differing submissions. The page also reports that GEMSDOE36
contains the champion plus additional dots; that claim has not been independently
pixel-compared in this review. It must not be treated as verified novelty.

Can a distinct model beat the supplied historical best? Possibly, but there is no
validated evidence here that it will. More physics or more dots is not by itself
an improvement. Fault mapping is the prediction task; vent discovery is a
possible application, not the evaluation target.
