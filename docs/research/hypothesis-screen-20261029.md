## What the existing evidence says about GEMSDOE32's 0.2778

The repository's pinned-byte audit reports that GEMSDOE32 B2 contains 37,654 dots and is a strict subset of the 44,090-dot GEMSDOE25 D2.8 raster: 6,436 dots were removed and none added. The audit attributes the removed dots to the 1–2-pixel neighbourhood of the catalogue in `labels.tif`. This is **raster lineage**, not proof that the files were uploaded or that pruning caused the score change. The 0.2778 value remains **OWNER-REPORTED** in this repo; no submission-page receipt is archived.

A plausible metric mechanism is reduced unsupported prediction mass: the local published-metric implementation uses alpha=0.2 for false positives, beta=0.8 for false negatives, and a 300 m triangular kernel. Pruning can help only if it removes predictions that contribute little hidden-fault credit; it can hurt if a held-out strand lies beside a mapped fault. The score mechanism is therefore plausible but not demonstrated by the leaderboard report. See [`why-02778.md`](why-02778.md) and [`focus9_content_checks_20261028.json`](../downloads/focus9_content_checks_20261028.json); the verifier is `scripts/verify_focus_content_20261028.py`.

## Preregistered experiment — candidate 1 only

- **ID / hypothesis:** `GSEWC36-EXP1`. Adding a fold-safe cross-sensor scale-space edge-width concordance feature to the frozen raw-19-band classifier improves whole-segment hide-and-recover prediction of catalogue faults.
- **Inputs:** the 19-band cached stack; new feature from only `14_tmi` and `13_iso_grav_anom`; 100 m pixel size. Use Gaussian derivative scales sigma={1,2,4,8} pixels and scale-normalized first-derivative magnitude `sigma * |grad(G_sigma * field)|`. For each field, retain peak response, peak scale, and peak normal. Normalize each sensor's peak by its own p99 over its valid four-sigma-eroded footprint support, clip to [0,1], then compute geometric-mean amplitude × `exp(-abs(log(sigma_mag/sigma_grav)))` × absolute normal-direction cosine. Nearest-valid fill is convolution-only; support excludes the four-sigma halo around missing cells and raster boundaries. No labels, catalogue distances, registry rasters, SGMC, vent/well records, or prior submission raster enter feature construction. Implementation: [`src/gemsdoe36/scale_space.py`](../../src/gemsdoe36/scale_space.py).
- **Leakage canary:** test each of the 19 raw features and the new feature individually, per fold, on held-out whole segments using the shared `feature_canary`; symmetric AUC >0.90 is a hard stop until leakage is explained. Do not use a feature with a failed canary.
- **Holdout:** use `scripts/evaluate_holdout.py`'s cached feature stack, `build_whole_segment_holdout_plan` (all 8-connected segments assigned whole to four spatial quadrants; 30-pixel / 3 km training collar; visible faults excluded from scoring pixel-exactly), and the shared `gems36-segment-audit-v1` evaluator. Keep the matched B1 raw-19-band HGB baseline, fold seeds, negative sampling, and 37,654-dot total budget fixed. Use the 300 m triangular DTI kernel with alpha=0.2 and beta=0.8; paired spatial-cell bootstrap for the candidate-minus-baseline interval.
- **Promotion criterion:** candidate must beat the matched B1 baseline with paired 95% CI strictly above zero and exceed the best recorded result for this exact evaluator (`HOLDOUT-DTI 0.114542`, 95% CI [0.078547, 0.151221], 60,988 withheld positives; `evidence/atvr36_run_card_20261026.json`). A score from another evaluator/buffer (including S2D-36 `gems-segment-pooled-v1`) is not a direct comparison. No leaderboard score is projected. The 0.2778 historical owner report is not a holdout target.
- **Registry and release:** compare the OOF prediction surface to the pinned registry before top-k placement; then compare the selected OOF dot set to registry rasters. The first literal violation stops the experiment; no threshold or registry class may be changed after results. The historical reg-052 five-pixel lattice is known to force a geometric lower bound of 82.3418% within-3-px overlap for any 37,654-dot footprint map (above the 70% limit); this is a degenerate calibration comparator, not evidence of actual geological duplication, but remains binding under the unrevised literal protocol. Thus this experiment may measure a research HOLDOUT-DTI result and an OOF overlap diagnostic, but it cannot create a final map or TIFF at the preregistered dot budget. Do not fetch later registry entries after a failing gate. No weekly slot can be selected by this experiment.
- **Budget:** one hypothesis, at most one model experiment, under two hours. Negative result is deliverable.

## GSEWC36-EXP1 outcome — negative; no submission artifact

The single preregistered experiment completed in **400.52 seconds**. All 19 raw bands and the candidate feature passed the per-fold feature-alone canary; the candidate's maximum two-polarity AUC was **0.523514** and the highest raw-band value was **0.602795**, both below 0.90. The new feature was computed from only bands `14_tmi` and `13_iso_grav_anom` (4,572,671 supported cells; 88.4912% of the footprint). The candidate feature transform itself reads no labels or registry rasters; shared cache/holdout construction did read the pinned label file as expected.

| Arm | HOLDOUT-DTI (`gems36-segment-audit-v1`) | 95% CI | Notes |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Matched B1 raw-19 HGB baseline | **0.039523** | [0.027195, 0.054596] | Reproduces the recorded baseline for the same evaluator. |
| B1 plus GSEWC36 feature | **0.039712** | [0.026757, 0.054990] | 60,988 withheld positives; 3,199 whole segments; 15 independent spatial cells. |
| Paired candidate − baseline | **+0.000189** | [−0.004191, +0.004111] | 2,000 paired spatial-cell bootstrap draws, seed 36; CI crosses zero (P(candidate > baseline) = 0.517). |

The candidate is **0.074830 below** the best recorded result for this exact evaluator (HOLDOUT-DTI 0.114542, 95% CI [0.078547, 0.151221]). It does **not** pass the scientific promotion criterion. These are internal holdout measurements, not organizer scores or leaderboard estimates.

The OOF prediction surface passed the first registry stop test against pinned `reg-052`: absolute Spearman `|rho| = 0.000325` (limit 0.90). After that surface check, 36,511 of 37,654 selected OOF dots (**96.9645%**) landed within 3 px of the five-pixel calibration lattice, exceeding the literal 70% stop gate. The actual OOF overlap is a research diagnostic, **not** a final-map overlap. Independently, the known lattice coverage gives a lower bound of 82.3418% for any 37,654-dot map, so the literal uniqueness gate cannot pass at this budget. The regular lattice is a degenerate comparator and this failure is not proof of geological duplication; nevertheless it remains binding. The registry scan stopped on this first failure; the other 197 registry entries were not checked.

**Verdict: NEGATIVE.** No full-map fit, final dot placement, submission TIFF/ZIP, submission name/note, validator run, or slot selection was made; `submission_writer.py` was deliberately not invoked.
