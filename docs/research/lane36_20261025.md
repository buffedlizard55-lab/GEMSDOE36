## Why h33-2-b2 scored 0.2778 (owner-reported; no organizer receipt in any repo)

Verified dot counts from the actual rasters (fetched 2026-10-25, SHA-checked mirrors):
h19-5 continuous surface 0.1922 → dotted d1.5 0.2477 → dotted d2.8 0.2600 (44,090 dots)
→ solo-d2.8 0.2708 (40,199 dots) → h33-2-b2 0.2778 (37,654 dots). Mechanism, from the
official metric (DrivenData problem page, fetched 2026-10-25): TP is a per-truth-pixel
MAX over a 300 m triangular kernel, FP is discounted by proximity to truth, α=0.2/β=0.8.
(i) Continuous lines waste budget — redundant pixels add FP mass but zero TP, so
Poisson thinning to ~280 m spacing is worth ≈ +0.07. (ii) Known catalogue faults are
excluded from hidden truth, so dots within 200 m of the catalogue are near-pure FP;
the B=2 flank prune (40,199→37,654) is worth ≈ +0.007. (iii) Every later variant that
only *added* dots to this core at its margin (GEMSDOE36 Anderson-PINN = core + 1,200
geothermal/step-over dots) scored 0.2750 ≤ 0.2778: the champion's surface (hand-weighted
h19-5 corroboration) is exhausted at its margin — the next dots it nominates hit hidden
faults at ≤ break-even rate (≈3 % at DTI≈0.28). Beating 0.2778 therefore requires a
*better-ranked surface*, not more emission engineering on the same surface. The current
public top (0.4413, owner-reported) implies leaders found a materially better surface
and/or density operating point; neither is reachable by re-thinning h19-5.

## Protocol (pre-registered before E2)
Hide-and-recover: connected catalogue segments assigned whole to 5 spatial strips;
500 m train buffer around withheld segments; catalogue used only as labels + B=2
emission mask (recomputed vs visible faults per fold); visible faults masked
pixel-exactly in scoring; pooled organizer DTI (metric.py) over 60,988 withheld
positives; segment-cluster bootstrap CI. Baselines: champion as-submitted
(handicapped: its B=2 used the full catalogue) and champion-analog (d2.8 with B=2
recomputed vs visible — the fair comparator). Lane gates: Spearman(surface, h19-5)
≤ 0.90 and dots-within-3 px ≤ 70 % vs every registry raster, checked on OOF surface
and final dots. Promotion gate: pooled HOLDOUT-DTI must beat champion-analog.
