### Why might the owner-reported 0.2778 have worked? Can a new approach beat it?

Given the cited historical reports (**D2.8 0.2600 → solo 0.2708 → B2 0.2778**), pruning predictions within 100 m and then 200 m of the known catalogue is consistent with lowering false-positive weight where catalogued faults may not qualify as *new*. The same D2.8 evidence produces the B2 mask exactly: it does **not** prove the remaining dots discovered hidden faults, prove a causal +0.0178 from removal, or validate the reported scores. Those inferences would require authenticated organizer receipts and access to the blind test labels. The [official problem/metric page](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/967/) and [rules PDF](https://docs.nlr.gov/docs/fy26osti/96647.pdf) are manual-review leads; external competition hosts could not be fetched from this sandbox, so local evaluator code is not a claim of exact portal equivalence.

In the repository's local implementation, the distance-tolerant score is a weighted Tversky index `TPw / (TPw + 0.2·FPw + 0.8·FNw)`, with 300 m triangular spatial credit. Controlling near-catalogue FP while **finding independently corroborated, off-catalogue truth beyond** the D2.8/B2 family is a scientifically plausible way above 0.2778—but **this run did not establish it**, and no score can be predicted from a holdout DTI. Prior reciprocal-score inversions suggesting a hidden truth count of ~12.6–12.9k pixels assume the owner-score/file pairings, the particular scoring regime and hidden prevalence; they are **conditional thought experiments**, not measured truth or a score forecast. The user's `0.3195` and `0.4413` describe incompatible/undated leaderboard targets; neither is a verified current standing here. [Check the live leaderboard yourself](https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/leaderboard/).

## One preregistered experiment, whole-segment spatial holdout

`E1` used `0.75 × orthogonal_crosscut + 0.25 × magnetic_termination`, compared with an **equal 37,654-dot-budget** raw RTP-edge control. The *same shared* `gems.holdout.SegmentHoldout` evaluator used four quadrants and **3,199 withheld whole fault components / 60,988 withheld positive pixels** across folds, with a **1,500 m buffer**, and rebuilt any catalogue features from visible faults only (this predictor uses none). Visible-fault pixels were masked pixel-exactly for holdout and output. The pooled TPw/FPw/FNw (not a mean of four fold scores) used α=0.2, β=0.8 and a Euclidean 300 m triangular kernel; 10 km spatial-block bootstrap produced the 95% CIs. This is `gems-segment-pooled-v1`, not an organizer score. Each of **nine** feature-alone two-polarity leakage checks passed, max AUC **0.5878** (<0.90).

## Submission guide for a *future promoted* experiment (not this TIFF)

1. The [official public reference solution](https://github.com/drivendataorg/gems-prize-reference-solution) documents a baseline; it does not authenticate any owner-reported sibling score.
2. Lock a distinct geological hypothesis and matched control **before** viewing holdout; rebuild catalogue features from visible faults only and mask known faults pixel-exactly. Require passing both strict all-entry novelty gates and a statistically positive holdout lift over the matched control *and* the recorded comparable group bar. Reject leakage canaries >0.90 AUC. Promotion remains separate from selecting a weekly live slot.
3. Use the shared writer to emit a one-band EPSG:32611 template-grid GeoTIFF, finite `[0,1]` across **all** pixels, and verify SHA-256, dot count, spacing, ZIP contents and local validators in a signed run card. Keep the metadata exact; do not relabel a negative candidate.
4. **Only if** the automated gate promotes a file and the selector approves a live slot under the *current* portal rules: download its exact TIFF, review the official instructions, upload it manually from an authorized account, use its ≤140-character note, and archive the actual organizer submission-page receipt. Then and only then label the resulting score `ORGANIZER-CONFIRMED`. This project never uploads autonomously or requests account credentials.
