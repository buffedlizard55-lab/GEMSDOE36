## Lane for a future experiment (one method only)

**Quality- and regime-weighted World Stress Map (WSM) orientation prior for the existing directional-structure score** is the sole lane selected for the next experiment. The novelty is using official, quality-ranked point stress observations and regime labels to estimate a spatially varying orientation distribution, rather than assigning a single regional extension direction.
