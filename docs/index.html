Why GEMSDOE32 h33-2-b2 scored 0.2778

Field

One strong field

Its 37,654 dots are an exact subset of 19GEMSDOE's 121,131-pixel H19-5 ridges (multi-line corroborated LiDAR openness, thermal/geochemical, tip/step-over and potential-field lines).

Emission

Dots, not lines

Thinning to ~3 px spacing (44,090 dots) lifted 0.1922 → 0.2600: each emitted pixel pays a 0.2 false-positive tax, while one dot credits all truth within 300 m.

Dead annulus

Deleting near-catalogue dots

Removing 6,436 dots within 200 m of mapped faults (B = 1, 2) lifted 0.2600 → 0.2708 → 0.2778. Those dots carried ~0.002–0.006 credit each vs 0.13 on average (hidden truth = new faults only).
